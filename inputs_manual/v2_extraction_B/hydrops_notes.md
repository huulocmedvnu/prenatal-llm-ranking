## Judgement calls (MAJOR DOUBT)
- The paper reports NO per-fetus ultrasound findings. The only imaging phenotype is the inclusion criterion (NIHF: >=2 of skin edema, ascites, pleural, pericardial effusion). Each included fetus got one row, "Nonimmune hydrops fetalis" -> HP:0001790 (exact label). The first 22 cases' detailed phenotypes are in Al-Kouatly 2021 (not used).
- "Unlikely" co-reported variants (COL2A1/FZD2/PLOD2, IDUA, TMEM107) were not recorded.
- NIHF1-018: the L-Path + VUS variants were recorded and the L-Benign c.5728G>A was dropped.
- GA = GA at diagnosis from Table 1 (cases 023-050 only).
